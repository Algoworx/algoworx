About Algoworx

Algoworx is an independent mobile games studio focused on building high quality, satisfying puzzle games for casual players. We believe great puzzle games should be easy to learn, deeply engaging, and completely fair — no pay to win, no dark patterns, just great gameplay.

Player First Games designed for enjoyment, not engagement metrics.
Fair Monetisation Clear advertising choices and optional rewards.
Privacy Respected Clear privacy information and control over advertising choices.
Quality Focused Small catalogue of polished games done right.
