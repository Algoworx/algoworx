Privacy Policy

UnityMobileStarter

Effective and last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy covers Algoworx’s mobile starter test application. It explains on-device saves, Google advertising and consent services, and how to contact us about privacy.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Algoworx provides UnityMobileStarter, a development and test application for mobile-game features, including advertisements, privacy choices, settings, and demonstration progress. Its Android application identifier is com.algoworx.unitymobilestarter.

This policy applies to UnityMobileStarter only. It does not describe GridCrush, other Algoworx games, or applications made by other developers using the starter.

Privacy contact: info@algoworx.com.

2. Settings and progress on your device

The app stores sound, music, and haptics preferences, volume settings, and demonstration progress in its local application storage. Local backups and recovery copies of save files may also be kept on the device.

Algoworx does not operate an account or cloud-save service for this test application. The app does not upload these save files to an Algoworx server.

The in-app controls let you reset settings and demonstration progress separately. To remove the app’s local files, including recovery copies, use Android’s controls to clear the app’s storage. Operating-system backup and restore behavior is managed by your device and platform settings.

3. Google advertising services

The AdMob-enabled test configuration uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display sample rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Sample ads are used for development testing; the app still communicates with Google’s services.

Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This can include:

  • Your device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as an advertising ID or app set ID, depending on SDK configuration and applicable privacy choices.
  • Interactions such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the app and SDK.

Google’s processing and retention practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy, Google’s information about partner apps and sites, and Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

4. Consent and advertising choices

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain current consent information and display privacy messages when required. The app uses UMP’s result to determine whether advertisements may be requested.

When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, the app displays a Privacy Options control so you can revisit your choices. Depending on your region and existing choices, a consent form or that control may not be shown.

You can also use the advertising privacy controls available in your Android settings to manage your advertising ID. Available controls depend on the Android version and device.

5. Contact information and privacy requests

If you email Algoworx, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message so we can respond.

For questions about this application, its privacy practices, or information you have provided directly to Algoworx, contact info@algoworx.com. For information processed by Google, its privacy policy explains Google’s controls and request procedures.

6. This policy webpage

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This policy page does not include advertising tags, analytics scripts, or third-party fonts.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its revision date. Please check the policy relevant to the version of the app you use.
