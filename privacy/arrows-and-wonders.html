Application privacy

Privacy Policy

Arrows & Wonders

Effective and last updated: 4 November 2026

Arrows & Wonders stores game progress and preferences on your device. When Google advertising and consent services are enabled, those services process information as explained below. For privacy questions, contact info@algoworx.com.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Algoworx provides Arrows & Wonders, a mobile puzzle game about clearing arrows and discovering illustrated worlds. Its Android application identifier is com.algoworx.arrowsandwonders.

The game is currently in development. This policy applies to its development and test builds and to releases using this application identifier. Features available in a particular build may differ. Advertising-related processing described here applies when those services are enabled.

This policy covers Arrows & Wonders only. Other Algoworx applications have their own privacy information.

Publisher and privacy contact: Algoworx, info@algoworx.com.

2. Progress and settings on your device

The app uses local application storage for progress and preferences. Depending on the features available in your build, this can include completed puzzles, scene discoveries, tutorial progress, sound and music settings, volume, haptics, and demonstration progress in development builds. Local backup and recovery copies of save files may also be kept on the device.

Algoworx does not currently provide a player account or cloud-save service for this game. These local save files are not uploaded to an Algoworx server.

You can use any reset controls available in your version. To remove the app’s local files, including local recovery copies, use Android’s controls to clear the app’s storage. Device backup and restore behavior is managed by your operating system and platform settings, and may restore previously backed-up data.

3. Advertising and Google services

When advertising is enabled, the app uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for rewarded and interstitial advertisements. The availability and placement of advertisements depend on the build and the game features in use.

Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. This includes the following categories, subject to SDK configuration and applicable privacy choices:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a general location.
  • Device and account identifiers, including an advertising ID or app set ID where available.
  • Interaction information, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the app and SDK.

Google states that data collected by the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google’s processing, sharing, and retention practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy, Google’s information about partner apps and sites, and Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

Some development builds simulate advertisements locally. AdMob test builds instead request Google’s sample advertisements; sample advertising can still involve communication with Google and the processing described above.

4. Consent and advertising choices

Builds using Google advertising use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to update consent information and display privacy messages when required. The app uses the service’s ad-eligibility result to determine whether advertisements may be requested.

When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, a Privacy Options control allows you to revisit your choices. Depending on your region and existing choices, a consent form or this control may not be shown.

Your choices can affect ad eligibility and the type of advertisements available. Declining personalised advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertisements.

You can also manage your advertising ID through the advertising privacy controls available in Android settings. The controls depend on your device and Android version.

5. Service providers and international processing

Google processes information in connection with the advertising and consent services described above. Information may be processed in countries outside the country where you live, as explained in Google’s privacy information.

If you contact us by email, your message is also processed through the email services used to deliver and handle that correspondence.

6. Retention and removal

Local progress, settings, and recovery files remain on your device until they are changed, replaced, or removed through the app or your device’s storage controls. Algoworx cannot retrieve local save files that have not been sent to us.

Support and privacy correspondence may be retained for as long as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal or legitimate business requirements. Google’s privacy information explains its own retention practices and available controls.

7. Contact and privacy requests

If you email Algoworx, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

For questions or requests concerning information you have provided to Algoworx, contact info@algoworx.com. Parents and guardians may also contact us about a child’s privacy. We may need information to verify and respond to a request.

For information processed by Google, its privacy policy describes Google’s controls and request procedures.

8. This policy webpage

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This policy page includes no advertising tags, analytics scripts, or third-party fonts.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its revision date when the game’s data practices change, including if new services or features introduce different processing. Please check the policy relevant to the version of the app you use.
